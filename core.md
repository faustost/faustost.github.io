## Micro-Product Strategy (Layering, not Switching)
**Philosophy:** Don't wait for a massive audience. Sell small things immediately to validate value and convert "Followers" into "Customers." Remember you won't find the ultimate project that leverages all your interests. But you can ship many that may do it together.

1.  **The "Sawdust" Rule (Ongoing):**
    *   Continue selling *competence* via free content.
    *   Every post validates authority ("Fausto understands the future").

2.  **The "Doorknob" Product (Start Now):**
    *   **Goal:** Break the psychological barrier of "I am just posting."
    *   **Price:** Low friction (R$ 47 - R$ 97 or "Pay what you want").
    *   **What:** Artifacts already created (e.g., Content Machine Notion template, "Top AI Tools for Public Servants" PDF, "Eurasia Game" source code).
    *   **Tactic:** Append as "P.S." on high-performing posts.

3.  **The "Inbound Signal" (High-Ticket):**
    *   **Trigger:** Wait for DMs asking for help/calls.
    *   **Action:** Offer limited consulting/mentoring spots.

## Content Flywheel (from creation to products):

1. **Start Small (Sawdust):** Tweet or short post to test idea
2. **Watch the Data:** See what generated engagement (likes, comments, questions)
3. **Expand What Works:** Viral tweet → Essay → Guide/E-book -> Course -> etc.
4. **Syndicate:** Same content adapted across platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter, Site, etc.)

Result: One idea becomes multiple assets → builds authority → feeds product sales

---

## Content Depth Pyramid (shallow to deep):

1. **Curation** — Aggregate value by organizing chaos ("Best AI tools for X")
2. **Obvious Connection** — "How to do X". Solves urgent, known problems
3. **Non-Obvious Connection** — Connect disparate dots (ex: Stoicism + AI Engineering)
4. **Category Creation** — Become reference in owned niche (eg: "Cognitive Sovereignty in AI Era")

## Engagement Strategy (The Human Engine):

**1. Audience Audition (Listen & Engage):**
- Find accounts worth engaging with.
- Add them to a private list (Twitter List/LinkedIn "Favorites").
- Comment strategy: Bring broader perspectives, talk about hidden details/concepts, or call attention to specific experts.
- Be useful or funny.

**2. Empower (Amplify others):**
- Retweet/Share when someone is struggling or celebrating.
- Boost their confidence and connect them with new people.
- Strategy: Curation is the first step to creating value.

---

## Owned vs Rented Land:

**Rented Land** (Discovery channels):
- Twitter/X, LinkedIn, etc.
- Use to: Find audience, test ideas, build initial authority
- Risk: Algorithm changes, account ban, no direct control

**Owned Land** (Asset channels):
- Newsletter, Email list, Site, Community
- Use to: Capture audience, build direct relationship, monetize directly
- Strategy: Use Rented Land → Move to Owned Land

**Transition Tactic:**
- Offer high-value resource (Guide, Template, Framework) in exchange for email
- Move 5-10% of audience from social → newsletter → course → consulting

# The Mission: Zen Tech

## The Core Insight

We are in the early stages of the most significant technological transition since the industrial revolution. AI and related technologies will reshape work, creativity, relationships, and what it means to be human.
**Most people will lose ground.**

Every piece of content, every post, every thread connects to one question:
> **How do we expand human agency in a time of rapid technological change?**

This isn't about:
- Hype ("AI will solve everything")
- Doom ("AI will destroy us")
- Grift ("Buy my AI course")

It's about:
- **Clarity**: Helping people understand what's actually happening
- **Capability**: Sharing tools, frameworks, and knowledge that expand what people can do
- **Agency**: Empowering people to be authors of their own lives, not passengers
